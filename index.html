Research objectives and model structure
OBJECTIVE 1

Representative cases

Harmonize crop × soil × climate × management.

OBJECTIVE 2

Evidence synthesis

Meta-analysis, moderators, priors, heterogeneity.

OBJECTIVE 3

Calibration + validation

Test skill, sensitivity, and uncertainty.

MODEL CORE

AquaCrop + response functions

Transparent, testable, reusable.

OBJECTIVE 4

Climate scenarios

24-GCM ensemble across historical simulations and four SSP pathways.

OUTPUTS

Decision evidence

Yield, water productivity, irrigation requirement, risk, climate information value.

OBJECTIVE 5

Decision support

Thresholds, trade-offs, user evaluation.
